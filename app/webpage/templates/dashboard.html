Calculations and details
  1. Purchase schedule

    Purchase interval = time from the last purchase to the next deposit ÷ (affordable purchases + 1), including estimated trading fees. Next purchase = last purchase + interval. Without a previous purchase, buying starts after startup checks. The final affordable purchase uses remaining fiat. Confirmed early deposits move planning to the next month; late deposits remain pending. Open transactions may delay execution.

    Automatic withdrawal

    __WITHDRAW_DESTINATION__ Automatic withdrawals use all available exchange BTC, including BTC bought outside the bot. When its value in the purchase currency reaches the threshold, the bot sends it minus withdrawal fees. Requires live trading.

    Automatic withdrawal forecast assumptions

    The forecast uses the current BTC balance, price, fees, purchase amount and expected monthly deposits on the configured day and time. Calendar month lengths and unused funds are included. The date estimates the final required purchase; withdrawal follows an eligible account check. Future prices, deposits and fees can change it. Countdown = estimated date minus now.

    Manual withdrawal

    __WITHDRAW_DESTINATION__

    After confirmation, sends available BTC minus withdrawal fees. Requires live trading; the automatic withdrawal switch and threshold do not apply. Only confirmed withdrawals qualify for export.

  2. Period analysis and Parqet export

    Current value prices all period purchases at the last checked BTC price, not the remaining wallet balance. Profit / loss = current value minus purchase costs and trading fees. Historical fiat amounts are converted to the display currency.

    The Parqet-readable CSV includes the complete cycle ending at the selected confirmed withdrawal, since its predecessor or the first recorded purchase. It uses EUR purchase costs and trading fees, and net BTC after withdrawal fees. Deposits, later purchases, Simulation and unconfirmed withdrawals are excluded.
