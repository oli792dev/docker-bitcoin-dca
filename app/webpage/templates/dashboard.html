Calculations and details
  1. Purchase schedule

    Interval = time from last purchase to deposit deadline ÷ (affordable purchases + 1), at least 1 minute. Fees are included. Next purchase = last purchase + interval. Buying starts after the startup delay and exchange check; the final purchase uses remaining fiat within exchange limits. Early confirmed deposits advance planning to next month; delayed deposits or open transactions can postpone purchases.

    Automatic withdrawal

    __WITHDRAW_DESTINATION__ At account checks, all available exchange BTC is valued in the purchase currency, including BTC bought outside the bot. Once the threshold is reached, it is sent minus withdrawal fees. Requires live trading and an unpaused bot.

    Automatic withdrawal forecast assumptions

    Uses the last checked balances and planned purchases, assuming unchanged price/fees and monthly deposits at the configured day/time. Month lengths and leftover funds are included. The estimate marks when the threshold could be reached; withdrawal follows an eligible account check. The deposit amount affects only this forecast.

    Manual withdrawal

    __WITHDRAW_DESTINATION__

    Confirm the preview to send available BTC minus withdrawal fees. Requires live trading, an unpaused bot, a configured target and a completed exchange check. Open transactions can block withdrawals. The automatic withdrawal switch and threshold do not apply. Only confirmed withdrawals qualify for export.

  2. Period analysis and Parqet export

    Totals exclude Simulation; fiat totals use your display currency. Invested = purchase costs + trading fees. Current value = BTC bought in the period × last checked price, including withdrawn BTC. Profit / loss = current value minus invested; withdrawal fees are separate. Net BTC subtracts confirmed and pending withdrawals, including BTC fees. Deposits are separate. Filtering activities does not change totals.

    Choose a confirmed withdrawal in the export dialog, independently of Analysis. The Parqet CSV combines purchases since the previous confirmed withdrawal (or the first recorded purchase) through the selected withdrawal into one Buy row. It uses EUR costs including trading fees and the selected withdrawal’s net BTC after fees. Requires a recorded real purchase; deposits, later purchases and Simulation are excluded.
