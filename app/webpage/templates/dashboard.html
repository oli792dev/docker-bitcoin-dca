Notes

  1. Schedule and automatic withdrawal

    Purchase interval = time from the last purchase to the next deposit date and time ÷ (affordable purchases + 1), including estimated trading fees. This places the final purchase before the deposit boundary. An overdue deposit remains pending until the exchange confirms a fiat deposit; usable old funds are spent first. Without a previous purchase, planning starts from now. Next purchase = last purchase + interval; elapsed time reduces the wait. Open orders block further purchases. Balances and BTC prices reflect the last account check.

    The automatic withdrawal threshold uses all available exchange BTC valued in the purchase currency, including BTC acquired outside this bot. Remaining purchases = (threshold ÷ current BTC price − available BTC) ÷ BTC per purchase, rounded up. The forecast projects these purchases using the current budget, price and order costs. Expected monthly deposit adds assumed funds on each configured deposit date and time; unused funds carry over and intervals are recalculated for actual calendar months. This affects only the forecast. The estimated date is the final required purchase; withdrawal follows an eligible bot check. If the threshold is already reached, the estimate is now. Countdown = estimated date − current time, with a minimum of zero. Estimates require automatic withdrawals and live trading; future prices, fees, deposits and execution may change them.

    Manual withdrawal

    Loading withdrawal configuration…

    After confirmation, sends all available BTC minus the exchange withdrawal fee. Requires live trading activated; the automatic withdrawal setting and threshold do not apply. Withdrawals appear immediately in Period activity. Only confirmed ones qualify for export.

  2. Period analysis and Parqet export

    Choose a custom range or an interval between withdrawals. Historical amounts are converted from their original currencies to the display currency.

    The Parqet-readable CSV exports only the complete cycle after the previous confirmed withdrawal through the last confirmed withdrawal in the selected range. It includes real purchase costs and trading fees, and the ending withdrawal’s net BTC after network fees. Later purchases, simulations and unconfirmed or failed withdrawals are excluded. A confirmed withdrawal and at least one real purchase in its cycle are required.
