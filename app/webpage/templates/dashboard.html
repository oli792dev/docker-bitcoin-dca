Calculations and details
  1. Purchase schedule

    Purchase interval = time from the last purchase to the planned deposit date and time ÷ (affordable purchases + 1). Affordability includes estimated trading fees. Next purchase = last purchase + interval. Without a previous purchase, buying starts after the startup delay and exchange check. The final purchase uses as much remaining fiat as exchange limits and rounding allow. Confirmed early deposits advance planning to the next month; overdue deposits remain pending. Open transactions can delay execution.

    Automatic withdrawal

    __WITHDRAW_DESTINATION__ At account checks, the bot compares all available exchange BTC × the exchange BTC price with the threshold in the purchase currency. This includes BTC acquired outside the bot. When eligible, it sends the available BTC minus withdrawal fees to the configured target. Requires live trading and automatic withdrawals enabled.

    Automatic withdrawal forecast assumptions

    The forecast projects purchases until the withdrawal threshold is reached, using the last checked balances, BTC price, estimated fees and purchase amount. Expected monthly deposits are assumed on the configured day and time; calendar month lengths and remaining funds are included. The deposit amount affects only this forecast. The date estimates the final required purchase, not a guaranteed transfer time. Withdrawal follows an eligible account check. Countdown = estimated date minus now, with a minimum of zero. Future prices, fees and deposits can change the estimate.

    Manual withdrawal

    __WITHDRAW_DESTINATION__

    After you confirm the preview, the bot requests a withdrawal of available BTC minus withdrawal fees. Live trading must be enabled, a target configured and the exchange check complete; unresolved transactions can block the request. The automatic withdrawal switch and threshold do not apply. Only confirmed withdrawals qualify for export.

  2. Period analysis and Parqet export

    Invested = purchase costs + trading fees. Current value = BTC bought in the period × the last checked BTC price, including BTC already withdrawn. Profit / loss = current value minus invested; withdrawal fees are separate. Net BTC = period purchases minus gross BTC withdrawals, including BTC withdrawal fees. Deposits are listed separately and do not count as invested. Historical fiat amounts are converted to the display currency.

    The Parqet-readable CSV exports one complete cycle: after the previous confirmed withdrawal through the selected confirmed withdrawal, or from the first recorded purchase if there is no predecessor. At least one real purchase in that cycle is required. The export uses EUR purchase costs and trading fees, and the ending withdrawal’s net BTC after withdrawal fees. Deposits, later purchases, Simulation and unconfirmed withdrawals are excluded. Choose the withdrawal interval in the export dialog; it can differ from the analysis period.
